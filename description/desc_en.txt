Send the open document for electronic signature under Brazilian law (MP 2.200-2/2001 and Lei 14.063/2020), without leaving LibreOffice.

The document is exported to PDF locally, sent to the SignDocs Brasil API, and each signer gets their own signing link. Works in Writer, Calc, Impress and Draw.

Requires a SignDocs Brasil account. Nothing leaves your machine until you confirm the send.
